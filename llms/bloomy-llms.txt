# Bloomy

> Bloomy (bloomylearning.com) is an AI-powered adaptive learning platform for K-12 students, backed by Y Combinator (Summer 2026). It diagnoses knowledge gaps and delivers personalized, standards-aligned paths across English Language Arts, Math, and Writing with Socratic AI tutoring and mastery-based practice. As of this profile Bloomy publishes no public API, developer portal, SDKs, or developer documentation.
